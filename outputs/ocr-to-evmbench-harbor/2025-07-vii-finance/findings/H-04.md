# [H-04] Floor-rounded liquidation transfers underpay liquidators

## Affected code

- `src/ERC721WrapperBase.sol`: `transfer` and `normalizedToFull`

## Root cause

`normalizedToFull` uses the default floor-rounding form of `Math.mulDiv` when
converting the liquidation's unit-of-account amount into ERC-6909 units. A
borrower can leave a one-unit token balance whose value later increases through
fee accrual; a partial liquidation then rounds the token transfer to zero even
though that token contributes material collateral value.

## Prerequisites

- The borrower enables multiple wrapped positions as collateral.
- One enabled `tokenId` is reduced to a very small ERC-6909 balance and later
  accrues meaningful LP fees.
- A liquidator performs a partial rather than full liquidation.

## Exploit sequence

1. The borrower unwraps or transfers all but one ERC-6909 unit of one enabled
   position.
2. Swaps or donations accrue fees to that underlying position, increasing the
   value represented by the remaining unit.
3. A partial liquidation requests a proportional transfer of the borrower's
   collateral.
4. `Math.mulDiv` floors the calculated ERC-6909 amount to zero for the valuable
   one-unit position.
5. The liquidator assumes debt without receiving the corresponding portion of
   that collateral value, while the violator retains it.

## Impact

Liquidators can suffer a direct collateral shortfall during partial
liquidation. The shortfall can be material when fees have substantially
increased the value represented by the residual ERC-6909 unit.

## Code evidence

At the audited commit, `ERC721WrapperBase.sol:176-178` calls the three-argument
`Math.mulDiv`, which rounds down. Fix commit
`5e825d5f2eee6789b646bd0f00e9a9a53b5039ca` changes the conversion to
`Math.mulDiv(..., Math.Rounding.Ceil)` so rounding favors the receiver, normally
the liquidator.

## Remediation

Round liquidation collateral transfers up in favor of the receiver and
consider guarding zero-value liquidations that can otherwise move indivisible
residual units.
