# [H-03] Full unwrap strands accrued Uniswap V4 fees after the position exits

## Affected code

- `src/ERC721WrapperBase.sol`: full `unwrap(address,uint256,address)`
- `src/uniswap/UniswapV4Wrapper.sol`: `tokensOwed`, `_accumulateFees`, and `_total`

## Root cause

The full unwrap overload burns the complete ERC-6909 supply and transfers the
underlying NFT without invoking any implementation-specific settlement hook.
For Uniswap V4, previously accumulated fee assets remain in the wrapper and in
`tokensOwed[tokenId]` after the last holder and the NFT have left.

## Prerequisites

- A Uniswap V4 position has accrued fees that were moved into the wrapper by a
  prior partial unwrap.
- The final ERC-6909 holder exits through the full unwrap overload.

## Failure sequence

1. A partial unwrap accumulates outstanding LP fees in the wrapper's currency
   balance and records them in `tokensOwed[tokenId]`.
2. The final holder later calls the three-argument full unwrap.
3. The base contract burns the remaining supply and transfers the NFT but does
   not settle the stored fees.
4. If the recipient burns the Uniswap position, that `tokenId` cannot be minted
   again, leaving the recorded fees without a recoverable claim path.

## Impact

The final holder or liquidator permanently loses accrued LP-fee assets, which
remain stuck in the wrapper contract.

## Code evidence

At the audited commit, `ERC721WrapperBase.sol:69-72` performs only `_burnFrom`
and `underlying.transferFrom` in the full unwrap. The V4 implementation tracks
fee assets in `tokensOwed` at `UniswapV4Wrapper.sol:34` and lines 224-227, but
the full unwrap never reads or clears that state. Fix commit
`bf5f099b5d73dbff8fa6d403cb54ee6474828ac4` introduces
`_settleFullUnwrap`, clears the fee record, and transfers both owed currencies.

## Remediation

Require every implementation to settle all position-specific state during a
full unwrap, and transfer and delete the V4 fee credits before the position
becomes unreachable.
